Portfolio Agent: a rule-based screener for my own holdings

A small local tool that merges holdings from two brokers and measures each position against thresholds I wrote down in advance. No AI opinions, no news, no orders.

Sample data only. Every company, number and signal below is made up to show how the screen behaves. This page connects to no broker and holds no real portfolio.

Why I built it

Holdings sat in two apps, and each one only told me what I owned, not whether it still met the standards I bought it on. So I wrote those standards down and built a small tool to measure every position against them.

  • One combined view. The same stock held in two places is merged into a single line with blended average cost.
  • My rules, written down. Thresholds live in one JSON file. A value set to null switches that check off. Change a number, re-run, see what flips.
  • Read-only by design. It can see holdings and nothing else. It runs on my own machine and the broker tokens never leave it.

How a signal is decided

  • Valuation, quality and growth are each a small set of pass/fail checks (P/E, ROE and ROCE, 3-year sales and profit growth). Two of the three groups must pass for the fundamentals to count.
  • Entry is a separate technical check: allowed trend, RSI inside a band, and not stretched above the 50-day average.
  • ENTRY ZONE means fundamentals pass and entry passes. WAIT FOR ENTRY means fundamentals pass but entry does not. TECHNICAL ONLY is the reverse. FAILS RULES means neither.
  • ETFs have no company fundamentals, so they skip those checks and are labelled as ETFs instead of failing.

Under the hood

  • A Next.js app bound to localhost only. Price history comes from public market data and fundamentals from a public company-data site, cached locally.
  • Holdings come from each broker's read-only holdings call. Sessions are stored locally and expire daily, so nothing long-lived sits on disk.
  • Exit and entry planners turn the same rules into staged levels (stops, tranches, targets). They are risk-control arithmetic, not predictions.
  • Adapted from an open-source Kite-based screener by VisahanA, rewritten for two Indian brokers with a combined view.
Illustrative sample data and an educational build project. Not investment advice, and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

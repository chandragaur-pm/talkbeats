Resources

Tools, links & community.

A working list of what's actually useful for learning and practicing product management.

Podcasts

  • The Investor's Podcast — We Study Billionaires
    Long-running show on investing, business models, and decision-making. Not PM-specific — useful for sharper business and capital-allocation thinking.

Videos — preparation & basics

  • AI Product Management (playlist)
    A structured playlist on how PMs scope, build, and ship AI-powered products — practical workflow and tooling, not model-building.

Blogs

  • Gaurav Tiwari — Fintech & Payments
    Personal blog from a fintech professional and storyteller, covering digital payments, entrepreneurship, and the lessons behind building in financial technology.
  • Shreyas Doshi — Why Product Sense Is the Only Skill That Matters in the AI Age
    As AI flattens tooling, a PM's lasting edge is product sense: the judgment to improve on what the model hands you.

Tools for learning

  • GrowthBook Blog
    Practical writing on experimentation, A/B testing, and feature flagging.
  • Mode
    SQL-based analytics and exploration — a good way to learn how PMs query and reason about data.
  • Ferrix AI — Agentic Product Management
    Agentic PM platform that unifies product signals, recommends what to build next, then drafts specs, tickets, and release plans for you to approve. Free in beta.

Community

  • r/ProductManagement_IN
    India-focused PM subreddit for hiring, salary, and career discussion.

Interview preparation

  • Product Management Exercises
    A large bank of PM interview questions and case studies with community-written answers.
  • Dossier — Interview Recon Agent
    Give it a company and role and it researches the web live, then hands back a one-page interview prep brief, the kind you'd put together the night before.

System design

  • Eigenvex
    AI-driven system design practice with company-calibrated rubrics and failure-mode feedback, modeled on how top tech companies grade architecture interviews. Login required.

Job portals

  • hackajob
    Tech hiring platform where companies reach out to you — sign up / apply.
